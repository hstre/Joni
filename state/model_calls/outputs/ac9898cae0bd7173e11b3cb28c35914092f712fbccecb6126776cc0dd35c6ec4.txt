["Existing detectors reward heads whose attended token matches the generated token, a literal-copy criterion that misses non-literal retrieval via the OV circuit.", "We introduce Logit-Contribution Scoring (LOCOS), scoring each head by the projection of its OV-circuit output onto the answer-token unembedding direction, contrasting needle and off-needle source positions in a single forward pass.", "Mean-ablating top LOCOS heads on NoLiMa collapses ROUGE-L at lower head counts than prior attention-based detections; on Qwen3-8B, ablating 50 heads drives ROUGE-L to 0.000 while the strongest baseline retains 0.292.", "The selected heads are retrieval-specific: parametric recall and arithmetic reasoning stay at baseline under the same ablation, while MuSiQue drops from 0.55 to 0.08 and BABI-Long from 0.62 to 0.20."]
