Recent video research tackles reconstruction from sparse event streams, few-step autoregressive generation, and interactive multi-target segmentation, while video game studies also appear in the literature. LongE2V applies video diffusion priors to event-based reconstruction to reduce texture blur and improve stability. Few-step autoregressive video generators accumulate errors and weaken motion dynamics, a problem OPSD-V addresses through on-policy self-distillation. Multi-target video segmentation commonly extends single-target methods by replicating processing for each individual target.
