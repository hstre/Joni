Research is creative and systematic work undertaken to increase knowledge by collecting, organizing, and analyzing evidence with attentiveness to controlling bias and error. In social psychological research, this includes careful conceptual distinctions, such as separating moderator and mediator variables. Exploratory research is aided by visualization systems like UCSF Chimera, which provide core services and extensible functionality. Video question answering research confronts the challenge of moving beyond black-box textual answers to achieve verifiable visual grounding.
